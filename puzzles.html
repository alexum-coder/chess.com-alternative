How puzzle rating works

  1. Solve the puzzle without making a wrong move to earn +5.
  2. A wrong move ends that attempt. You can retry and finish the puzzle, but it won't award rating.
  3. Hints and the solution arrow are available whenever you need help. They do not remove the rating reward.
